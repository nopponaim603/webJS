VOLTA: Lineman of the Storm

Creator age rating · 13+

VOLTA is a one-finger swing arcade on live power lines ahead of a thunderstorm, painted like a 1930s WPA poster. Hold to throw your cable to the best insulator ahead and swing while the winch reels you in; release to fly. Every tower crackles for a second before lightning hits it, then the current runs down every cable on that tower: if your line is still on it, you fry. Let go a beat early for an ARC DODGE bonus. Collect insulators along your swing arcs for combo score and rubber gloves for one free shock. The storm wall never stops: outrun it through five hand-drawn sections (Prairie, The Gorge, Rail Yard, The Pass, Night Line), then endless harder loops. Every death is readable: the line went live, gravity won, the storm took the line. PixiJS 8, flat three-colour poster art, scratched rain, baked paper grain, procedural thunder and crackle. Same render on phone and desktop.
